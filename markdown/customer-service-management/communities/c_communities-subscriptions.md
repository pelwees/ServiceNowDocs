# Community subscriptions

Using subscriptions, you can choose specific areas of interest. Your activity feed and notifications are personalized according to your subscriptions.

<table id="table_yjx_2k5_vz"><thead><tr><th>

Subscription type

</th><th>

Description/Features

</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>

Subscription

</td><td>

-   Subscribe or unsubscribe to forums, topics, and community content \(questions, answers, blogs, and video\).
-   Auto-subscribe: Whenever you post content, you are automatically subscribed to the content itself. You are also automatically subscribed when you post a reply or a comment.

</td></tr><tr><td>

Activity feed

</td><td>

Displays activities based on your subscriptions with the most recent at the top.

</td></tr><tr><td>

Notification settings

</td><td>

-   Manage your subscriptions using **Notification Settings**.
-   For each of your subscriptions, set email notifications using **Notification Preferences**.

</td></tr></tbody>
</table>-   **[Subscribe to content](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/customer-service-management/communities/../task/subscribe.md)**  
Subscribe to forums, topics, questions, blogs, and videos. You are automatically subscribed to content that you create.
-   **[Set notification preferences](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/customer-service-management/communities/../task/manage-subscriptions.md)**  
Set notification preferences to receive updates via email on the content that you are following.
