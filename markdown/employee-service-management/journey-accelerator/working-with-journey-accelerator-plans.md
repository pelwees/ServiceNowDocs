# Working with Journey Accelerator plans

Managers, employees, and mentors can access the Journey Accelerator plans through the Employee Center \(EC\).

For information about the EC, see [Employee Center with HR Service Delivery](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/employee-center-hr.md) and [Employee Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/../../employee-center/concept/employee-center-landing-page.md).

-   **[Monitor Journey Accelerator plans](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/../task/monitor-journey-accelerator-plans.md)**  
Monitor plans and to-do tasks from the Journey Accelerator dashboard.
-   **[Create and track plans for your employees](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/../task/create-employee-plan.md)**  
Create and manage employee transition plans by using Journey Accelerator templates. You can use role-based plans to create consistent plans for different roles on your team.
-   **[Employees view and complete action plans](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/../task/ja-employee-plan.md)**  
View, manage, and track your action plan progress from the Employee Center portal.
-   **[Mentor employees by using a Journey Accelerator plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/journey-accelerator/../task/ja-mentor-plan.md)**  
Mentor employees that are assigned to you and view or edit employee plans depending on your permission type.
