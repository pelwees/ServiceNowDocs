# Working with Schedule Planning

Create and execute schedule plans to assign workspaces to your employees.

-   **[Create a schedule plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/workplace-central/../task/create-schedule-plan.md)**  
Create a schedule plan to assign employees, spaces, and employee-space mappings for your projects.
-   **[Edit a schedule plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/workplace-central/../task/edit-schedule-plan.md)**  
Edit a schedule plan to change the details, selected users, or selected spaces.
-   **[Cancel a schedule plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pelwees/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/workplace-central/../task/cancel-schedule-plan.md)**  
Cancel a schedule plan to deactivate the location assignment records for your employees.
